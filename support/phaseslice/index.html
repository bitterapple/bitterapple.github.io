PhaseSlice

Released — Version 1.2.1.

5-band split-delay and phase-shaping plug-in

PhaseSlice is a macOS plug-in (AAX / AU / VST3) that goes one step past a band-pass filter: instead of simply isolating a frequency range, it lets you delay only that band and then blend it back with per-band mix, trim, polarity, and phase control.

What it does

  • Split the signal into 5 crossover-based bands that sum back together cleanly.
  • Delay each band independently with Delay, Mix, and Trim controls.
  • Delay time supports up to 50 ms with 0.01 ms control resolution.
  • Resizable window support is included for a more flexible editing layout.
  • Shape interference and depth with Polarity and All-pass phase amount per band.
  • Keep the result practical with output trim, limiter, auto gain compensation, and delay smoothing options.

Interface overview

Top section

FFT display, crossover markers, and a delay profile view so the band timing shape is visible at a glance.

Bottom section

Focused editing for the selected band: delay, mix, trim, phase, polarity, solo, and mute.

Typical use cases

Kick shaping

PhaseSlice is especially useful on kick drums when you want to reshape the apparent weight, click, or front-edge timing of different frequency areas without simply boosting EQ.

Single-drum design

It also works well on individual drum hits such as snares, toms, or percussion when you want tighter low-end alignment, sharper attack focus, or slightly unusual phase-based texture.

While it can be used more experimentally on other material, the most practical starting point is usually drum sound shaping rather than full-mix processing.
