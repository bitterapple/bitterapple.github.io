DSPTrace is an in-development analysis plug-in for loading AU and VST plug-ins, automatically sweeping their parameters, and measuring how those changes affect the signal. The goal is to make a plug-in’s behavior easier to characterize in a repeatable way, instead of relying only on manual listening and guesswork.

The current build is still in progress, but the direction is clear: faster plug-in analysis, clearer parameter-to-sound tracing, and a better way to compare how processors actually behave.
