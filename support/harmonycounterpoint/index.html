Harmony & Counterpoint

Available

Guided Harmony and Counterpoint Practice

Harmony & Counterpoint is a music learning app for harmony and counterpoint practice, with guided exercises, easier note entry, daily questions, and red-pen style review. It is designed to make written music theory feel like something you can practice, check, and improve.

← All apps View on the App Store Contact support

What it does

  • Level assessment: Helps learners identify a clear starting point.
  • Goal-based study planning: Build a learning path that matches the user’s current level and goals.
  • Daily question and reminders: Encourage steady, repeatable practice over time.
  • Easier note entry: Improves input so writing music feels faster and more natural.
  • Harmony and counterpoint exercises: Practice core skills directly in the app.
