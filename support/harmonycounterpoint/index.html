Harmony & Counterpoint

Available

Music Theory, Ear Training & Composition Practice — Version 2.4

Harmony & Counterpoint is a complete music theory training app for composers, musicians, and students. Build practical skills in harmony, counterpoint, ear training, instrumentation, orchestration, solfège, and jazz through interactive exercises, notation input, and answer-grounded feedback.

← All apps View on the App Store Contact support

Learn the language of music

Follow a connected path from hearing and understanding music to writing, arranging, and checking your own work.

  • Harmony and counterpoint: Work with chord function, voice leading, and independent melodic lines directly in notation.
  • Ear training and solfège: Connect what you hear and sing with the notes you write through progressively structured practice.
  • Instrumentation and orchestration: Study register, playable range, instrumental roles, and real-score listening examples.
  • Jazz study: Explore chord symbols, extensions, tensions, functional harmony, and improvisation foundations alongside classical practice.
  • Guided daily practice: Use level assessment, goals, study planning, daily questions, and reminders to keep making progress.
  • Feedback you can use: Review written answers with clear, notation-based corrections instead of treating practice as a quiz alone.
