Calendar Calendar

New

A macOS calendar widget with customizable colors and fonts.

Build a calendar view that matches your desktop style. Tune colors, switch fonts, and keep dates readable at a glance.

← All apps App Store Contact support

What it does

  • Shows a compact monthly calendar on your macOS desktop.
  • Supports visual customization without changing system settings.
  • Keeps weekday/weekend hierarchy clear with theme controls.

Appearance settings

Color

  • Accent: Selected date and highlights.
  • Background: Widget panel tone.
  • Weekday text: Monday-Friday color.
  • Weekend text: Saturday/Sunday color.
  • Today ring: Outline color for current day.

Font

  • Family: Choose your preferred typeface.
  • Weight: Lighter or stronger text style.
  • Size: Better readability at a distance.
  • Header style: Month/year text appearance.
  • Spacing: Control visual density.
