Free iPad Sheet Music Library

ScoreShelf is a free iPad app for keeping sheet music PDFs in one place, opening them quickly, and adding light handwritten notes while you practice. It is designed around a practical score library: import PDFs, jump back to useful pages, mark practice spots, and keep a small history of what you worked on.

What it does

  • Import and organize sheet music PDFs for reading on iPad.
  • Open scores in a focused reader with Apple Pencil markup for small notes, reminders, and practice markings.
  • Save bookmarks for pages you need to return to quickly.
  • Create Practice Spots for passages that need repeated work.
  • Track recent practice time by score so the library reflects what you are actually working on.
