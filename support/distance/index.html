Distance

Version 1.0.0 — Out now.

Distance Perception Shaping Plug-in

Distance is a macOS audio plug-in (AAX / AU / VST3) designed to change the perceived distance of a sound source in a more natural way than simple reverb or EQ. When you want something to feel a few meters farther away, traditional tools often miss the mark. It is built for a subtle kind of depth control: in some mixes the change may feel small at first, but when that slight front-to-back shift is exactly what you need, Distance can be surprisingly useful. This first release is just the starting point, and the algorithm will keep improving over time.

What it does

  • Change the perceived distance of a source without relying on reverb alone.
  • Help sounds feel farther away or closer in a way that is more convincing than simple tonal darkening.
  • Aim for subtle, mix-friendly depth control that can sit naturally inside a real mix.
  • Be especially useful when a source needs to move just a little in depth, even if the difference is not dramatic on every sound.
  • Focus on being a practical utility plug-in rather than a flashy special effect, with future algorithm improvements still planned.

Typical use cases

Vocals

Move a vocal slightly back in the mix without relying on an obvious reverb tail.

Dialogue / FX

Help dialogue, foley, or effects feel a few meters farther away in a more believable way.

Mix depth

Create clearer front-to-back placement when EQ and reverb alone do not quite get there.

Install

  1. Download Distance_1.0.0.pkg.
  2. Run the installer to place AAX / AU / VST3 into the standard macOS plug-in locations.
  3. Launch your host application and rescan plug-ins if needed.
