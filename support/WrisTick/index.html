WrisTick

WrisTick is a watchOS metronome and tuner for musicians who want tempo, haptics, tap tempo, and pitch reference on their wrist.
Stay in time on your wrist with metronome sound, light, and haptic feedback, plus Tap Tempo, drag BPM, and a quick tuner with A4 and sharp/flat support.

Quick answers

  • Metronome: Open WrisTick. The main screen is the metronome.
  • Tuner: Long-press TAP TEMPO to open the tuner.
  • Sound settings: Long-press the speaker icon.
  • Flash settings: Long-press the light-bulb icon.
  • Haptic settings: Long-press the hand icon.
