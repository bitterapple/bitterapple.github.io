What is TakeFlow?

TakeFlow is an app-based recording workflow for narration, voice-over, game dialogue, e-learning, short-form ads, and other spoken-word projects.

For people requesting voice work

The requester side turns a script or spreadsheet into a trackable recording job, then keeps every review decision tied to the exact line.

Requester limits

For performers accepting the work

The performer side stays focused on the assigned lines, recording context, review notes, and the next take that needs to be submitted.

How the project moves between both sides

TakeFlow keeps the collaboration explicit: structure is created by the requester, recordings arrive from the performer, and review decisions flow back as line-level status.
